AAMs are also centres for community health outreach for Community Health Workers and
Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs). At these centres, they spread awareness of health
programmes, check in on patients, and keep track of community health.
Improving Public Health at the Grassroot Level
For years, the Lalmati sub-centre in Assam's Bongaigaon district served over 10,000 people across 10
villages, providing only basic maternal and child health services. When the centre was upgraded to an
AAM through the Ayushman Bharat scheme, it was truly transformed. The AAM expanded its
services, adding screening of non-communicable diseases, mental health support, oral and eye care,
and emergency services. Trained Community Health Officers were deployed to lead the team
delivering these services.
Due to these changes, the area has recorded zero maternal and child mortality from preventable
causes since 2024. High-risk pregnancies have more than halved. Cases of communicable diseases
and anaemia have also declined.
